# Family: crisis-diagnostic — Crisis-era diagnostics and withdrawn options

During the leak crisis the project minted organising interpretations — the single-mismatch hypothesis, the Reading-A ladder, the transient bug attributions — each organised the findings and each was superseded by the C1/C2/C3/C4 verdicts and the k=1 reframe — the interpretations cost debugging time but bought the discipline of splitting hypotheses from cures; keep the facts (the falsifications are live), retire the framings (they are history), and never let a diagnostic with no falsifiable prediction ride the register again.
