# Family: ruleset-choice — Ruleset-choice foreclosures — why k=1 won

The project measured and foreclosed every generation rule except k=1 — PSK exact-solve is intractable (118,475,182 / 116,114,272 ban-set states), score-on-cycle is byte-identical to it, kill-X% makes the region worse, N-ply is intractable for every N — the k=1 game (basic ko, TIE=0) is the one that could be solved; the foreclosures cost weeks of census and search that never produced a table, and must not be re-litigated: each is a recorded decision with its measurement, and R's axioms (B1) already encode the winner.
