## What the FAILs have in common

Every FAIL sits in **prose that re-quotes another run's number at a
distance**, not in a measurement table. The tables themselves — chainability,
reachcensus, psk-binding, arena persona sweeps, the censuses — are essentially
clean: 100% of their *in-table* percentages pass (the one in-table exception
is `scaling-census.md:15`, a one-character truncation). The failure modes are,
from mildest to worst: (1) **guard-digit sloppiness** in hand-computed
corrections (22.72/22.71; 60.53→60; the 3-significant-figure "21.3%"
truncation); (2) **denominator-set drift** when a figure crosses a document
boundary — the 21.27/21.32/21.33 trio travelled from the census into
narrative docs and lost its subscript, which is precisely the confusion the
chainability doc's reconciliation exists to end; (3) **unverifiable numerators
in retrospective prose** — figures written from memory of uncommitted runs
(2×2/3×2/3×3 arena ranges, the 49% upper bound, the 8–18% "T06 band", the
decile histogram); and (4) one **units slip** where a named denominator was
replaced by a handier one (`positions` for `(b,side)` triples at
kostate:224) — the only FAIL that flips a conclusion. Single-board vs
cross-board splits don't matter; L==H vs ko-sensitive doesn't matter. Distance
from the run, and prose-vs-table, is the whole story.
