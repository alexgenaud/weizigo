## 3. What the fix makes unnecessary

The hand annotations this census relied on (`DO NOT SCORE` for the five
Claude rows; the 12-row cohort) were written by hand because the tooling
could not distinguish. After T625/D021, a directive kill records
`verified=directive-kill reason=<id>/<type>`, a real refusal records
`verified=unreached reason=provider-429` (with the `hit your session limit`
signature and the windowed scan), and a harness kill with only a quoted
refusal records `verified=fail` with the run record's `killed` reason.
Each of the seven recoverable rows above now classifies correctly under the
shipped classifier (verified against the on-disk fixtures).
