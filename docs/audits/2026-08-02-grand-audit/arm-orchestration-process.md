## (b) Systemic weaknesses

**B1. Process churn dominates throughput ~3:1 by commits, ~6:1 by file-touches.** Since 2026-07-22: 322 commits; **228 (71%) touch no `src/` file at all**. File-touch events: docs/ 1,318 vs src/ 213. Top churn: `docs/infra/managent/tasks.json` — **153 commits, 47% of every commit in the window** — followed by model-perf.md (38), CURRENT.md (21), CLAIMS.md (21). The kanban's single-writer-binary design forces a commit per state change, so bookkeeping consumes half the commit history. Against the standing goal ("least friction, no ceremony"): the *rules* are mostly disaster-earned (see (c)), but the *bookkeeping cost per unit of engine work* is very high, and the docs corpus (13.6k words of role/delegation docs alone, 4,684 lines of infra process docs) is a per-boot tax paid by every mortal agent on every restart.

**B2. The channel accumulates because the gate can never open.** One live epic channel (rule "max two live" trivially satisfied), but it holds **99 files, 584 KB, ~89,168 words** since 2026-07-28, with residue items from the 2026-07-28 promotion pass (DECISIONS.md:31-54) still open. A deletion gate conditioned on "every decision promoted" plus an epic-close gate means, in practice, the channel is immortal and grows monotonically — while remaining git-ignored and clone-fragile.

**B3. Two coexisting lock mechanisms.** dispatch/README.md:67-70 mandates prose ownership declarations in CURRENT.md; ROLES.md:92-97 says `holds=` in managent "is the concurrency gate." CURRENT.md:182-186 shows the prose mechanism was used and released (T164, register catch-up) — so not pure theater — but it depends on a file that (A3) goes stale, while `holds=` is mechanically enforced. No recorded write collision on engine files since the mechanism landed; the one recorded kanban collision (`009-opus-to-glm.md:5` "exactly the collision the milestone protocol exists to prevent, and I caused it" — the Orchestrator itself; Dabir's `011:12` "it cost us a kanban collision and a relayed-by-hand dispatch") predates managent's serialization and motivated D-8. Verdict: locks are real but redundant; the prose variant should be retired.

**B4. Hand-ferry failure modes are recorded, and fixes are a mix of root-cause and ritual.** Concrete recorded failures:
- **EXP-2B/B1 thrash ×3**: `041-dabir-to-all.md:28-29` — EXP-2B re-dispatched to kimi-k2.7 "ran the brute a **third time** and was killed"; the 10h22m `qa023_brute_2x2` thrash recorded as a lesson (`020-orchestrator-to-all.md:72`). Root cause: a re-dispatched console can't see that the state advanced (2B-PROBE-FIX done, C2 falsified) — a state-sync failure inherent to hand-ferry. Fix applied: STATE.md-first discipline + runner budgets — mitigates, doesn't remove the sync gap.
- **Silently broken dispatch path**: STATE.md:80-82 — the documented `odeeppi`/`oflashpi` aliases "are interactive-shell only and **had silently blocked every dispatch**." The project's subdelegation doc prescribed commands that never worked in context; fixed only when someone dogfooded (`subdelegation.md` now says `bin/subagent`).
- **Instruments lying**: ORCHESTRATOR.md:40 — on 2026-08-01 "three instruments, all lying, all found by running the cadence once"; STATE.md:44-46 — "**every battery output before 2026-08-02 came from a stub returning `skipped`**" (CODE.BATTERY-STUBBED). An entire verification sprint's outputs were theater until T258.
- **QA-023**: five audit links passed a defective artifact (AGENTS.md:133-155). The fixes are standing *rules*; the only mechanism that has ever found real defects here is independent re-implementation (AGENTS.md:150-152), which is a practice, not an enforcement.
- **Evidence destruction** (T13, B44 sweep) — root-caused properly: evidence-in-git rule + the two-directory doctrine (AGENTS.md:174-195) + claimlint C2 checks. This one is a genuine root-cause fix.

Judgment: the architecture's irreducible weakness — one human as the only bus, with no atomicity and no delivery guarantee — is acknowledged repeatedly (SPEC-msgbus, msg 016) but the messaging fixes stall at spec stage, while rule-text accumulates in AGENTS.md. Rules that mechanized (managent verdicts, claimlint floors, runner caps, done-checks-deliverables) stuck; rules that stayed prose (numbering, brief header, read orders) drifted immediately.

**B5. Role docs are individually good, collectively over-engineered for a one-human bus.** Seven seats operated in five days (Orchestrator, Dabir, Auditor, Argus, Consul, Navigator, workers) with 4 role docs + 3 delegation docs + ROLES.md §restraint/§instance-vs-model essays. Every seat transition costs a handover doc (`docs/status/handover-*.md`, 5 of them) and a re-read of the chain. The human can only ferry so many consoles; the seat count exceeds the bus bandwidth, and the registry (A1) can't keep up with seat invention — strong evidence the role system has outgrown its transport layer.

## (c) What genuinely works

- **managent** is a real, verified tool: 47 tasks with clean lifecycle (40 done, 6 dispatchable, statuses queryable), `holds=`-based mechanical concurrency, `needs=` dependencies, done-refuses-without-attribution (ORCHESTRATOR.md:41), done-checks-deliverables-exist (DELEGATEE.md:77-79), terminal verdict vocabulary fixing a real information loss (D-22: T193's failure had been recorded identically to a pass). The kanban-disagrees-with-reality-is-the-bug stance (ROLES.md:111) is the right inversion.
- **The analysis-parallel / mutation-serial doctrine** (ROLES.md:70-100) is simple, correct, and honestly concludes "throughput is set by the mutation queue, not the agent count." ROLES.md also explicitly claims concurrency authority and disarms stale "max two" caps elsewhere — a rare case of contradiction-resolution done right.
- **The QA-018 three-seat blind panel protocol** (`QA-018-REVIEW-PANEL.md`) is excellent parallel-review engineering: concurrent blind seats, exactly-two-files write isolation, pre-assigned message numbers to defeat the numbering race, kanban serialized through the Orchestrator, disagreement-as-signal. It produced a unanimous verdict the human could rule on.
- **The instance-vs-model independence doctrine** (ROLES.md:136-163) — procedural vs epistemic independence, correlated-error from shared training, disclose-same-model — is more sophisticated than most industry practice.
- **STATE.md discipline held at the last shutdown**: updated at 04:03 2026-08-02, the exact timestamp of HEAD `e714fd4`, with an accurate queue matching tasks.json (T266/267/268/269/270/T203 all dispatchable). The crash anchor works — when someone remembers CURRENT.md too (A3).
- **model-perf.md is genuinely maintained** (dated per-model entries with costs, context windows, task-shaped strengths; session absorption through 2026-08-01) and does support the allocation policy — DSPro/Flash defaults, Opus/Fable reservations trace to D-7 and observed behavior, not vibes.
- **Disaster→rule provenance is real, not cargo cult.** Nearly every heavy rule cites its incident: runner ← kernel panic (`docs/infra/host/incident-2026-07-29.md`); evidence-in-git ← T13; copy/paste fences ← truncated relays; verify-then-promote ← QA-023; untracked two-type split ← B44. Dabir's own principle "retire nothing that prevents a known failure mode" (DABIR.md:30 — sprint.md retired, rediscovered 9 days later, 4 defects in the gap) is the honest counterweight to "no ceremony."
- **The self-audit reflex works**: the 2026-08-01 coherence audit found the read-first surfaces stale, the 2026-08-02 session found four broken instruments in one day by running the cadence — the process detects its own rot, even if it re-rots fast.

**Bottom line for synthesis**: the epistemic machinery (register, claimlint, evidence rules, blind panels, independent re-implementation) has demonstrably caught real defects and is the project's crown jewel. The *coordination* machinery is oversized for its transport: a single human hand-ferrying between 7+ seats, with the recorded transport fixes (msgbus) unimplemented, message numbers colliding for five straight weeks, half of all commits spent on kanban bookkeeping, and the durable resume surface (CURRENT.md) stale within 24 hours of a remediation aimed at exactly that. Process is not yet the goal — most ceremony is scar tissue with a citable wound — but the ratio (71% non-code commits, 6:1 docs:src churn, 37k-word worker onboarding for dispatch consoles) says the court now costs more to convene than the bench work it adjudicates.
