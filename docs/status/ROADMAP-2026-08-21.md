## Measurement rules (recorded, not deferred)

- **Quality and allocation are two verdicts, never one "winner" line.** Cost is out of the scored metrics
  (D35); B-3 is a tie on result quality (D34). A cost-derived tie-break recorded as a race win teaches the
  ladder a billing fact as a capability fact.
- **A flag name is not a capability.** The lane transcript is the oracle (D33/§9 of the methodology).
- **Review of a race needs the same non-family independence as its grading** (methodology §5): whoever
  re-derives a race result must not be same-family as the lane it ranks, or the ladder learns self-assessment.
- **A race's value is the union, not the ranking** — 7 models → 41 findings, best single → 21, the four
  criticals each from a different model. Capability is two separable axes (verification depth vs code
  throughput; qwen is a verification specialist, its Zig is *untested* not *absent*).
- **Define the home first.** No flat piles; establish the layout before running (the directory refactor is
  the structural fix; the habit is the rule). The B-3 handover (brief + committed state → clean T557 pickup)
  is the proof the end-state — tooling + brief, not a long-lived seat — is reachable.
