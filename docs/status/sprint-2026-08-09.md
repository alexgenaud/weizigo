### What I sent back / superseded — the D058 finding (the audit's load-bearing catch)

**The operator refined the sibling test to a cycle test (D058), posted 41 seconds after T412
closed and never read by the worker.** D058 says the per-position count (every optimal child
loopy) is the *wrong primary test* — "it can flag a position that no optimal line ever
sustains". The right test is: build the optimal-move subgraph (all optimal children, keeping
ALL ties) and ask whether it **contains a cycle**. T412 measured exactly what D056 asked, and
every number reproduces — but its headline conclusion ("the operator's no-problem case is
falsified") **outruns the evidence as the operator refined it**: the per-position count is now
the secondary descriptive reading, and the cycle result is the headline. I therefore do NOT
propagate "falsified" as established: the honest statement is that the *necessary condition*
for the no-problem case fails (positions exist where optimal play must enter the loopy region),
while whether optimal play can *sustain* a loop — the operator's actual question — **remains
open pending the cycle test**. Also honestly not done: the quasi-rule side-by-side (no
no-capture artifact exists) and 4×3 DTT (no WZO2).

**Landmark:** advances `L2 (proven 4×4 values)` — the loop phenomenon is now located
(per-position) and the ply cap is measured from the table, where before it was only inferred
from a 96 % cap rate. What remains: the cycle test (D058) — the operator's refined headline —
