## Stage 4 — the clean story (operator directive 2026-08-20; after L1, its own console + sprint)

The operator's ask, verbatim in intent: clean up the claims and history, eliminate the kludge
and confusing dead ends — **not the falsifications** — then squash history to many fewer
commits. Precedent exists: the 2026-07-22 squash to 10 commits. This is registered as a
spec-first console sprint (spec → independent audit → mechanical rehearsal in a clone →
execute), **not** dispatched piecemeal, because history rewrite is the least reversible
operation this project performs. Constraints, fixed now so the spec cannot drift:

1. **The archive branch comes first and survives forever.** `archive/pre-squash-2026-08-20`
   (pushed, sha `b389b0f`) is the complete record; the squash rewrites `main` only. Squash
   plus archive *is* archive-never-delete; squash without archive is destruction.
2. **Falsifications and honest negative results are content, not kludge.** The rubric the spec
   must draw: a *dead end* is work whose recorded lesson is preserved in the register or an
   epitaph (then the noise can go); *kludge* is scaffolding nothing cites. Falsified claims,
   refuted hypotheses, and the corrections-not-to-remake stay — doubt-everything depends on
   them.
3. **The evidence chain is hash-addressed.** CLAIMS.md, findings, and audits carry
   commit-pinned refs; a squash breaks every one. The spec must ship a mechanical old→new
   hash remap (committed as a table) plus an evidence-repoint pass, and claimlint must read
   green (C2 not worse, C10 not worse) before AND after — measured, not asserted. This is why
   Stage 4 runs best **after** Stage 3.4's ledger-to-zero: every dead link fixed beforehand
   is one fewer to remap.
4. **The fleet is drained during the rewrite.** No console holds a worktree across a
   rewritten main. One writer, stated cutover, same discipline as a binary cutover.
5. **Landmark home:** candidate `L10 (the repository tells its story cleanly)` — proposed for
   D4-style adoption; folding it into `L4 (the ledger is clean)` is the fallback if the
   operator prefers no new map row. The seat presents the choice with the Stage-4 spec.
